🔸 How often do you go to the supermarket?
👉 I go to the supermarket once/twice/three times a week...
 
Tradutor: I usually go to the supermarket once a month, but sometimes I go once a week to buy small things.
IPA: /aɪ ˈjuːʒuəli ɡoʊ tə ðə ˈsuːpərˌmɑrkɪt wʌns ə mʌnθ, bət ˈsʌmtaɪmz aɪ ɡoʊ wʌns ə wiːk tə baɪ smɔl θɪŋz/

🔸 What do you usually buy?
👉 I usually buy ________. (milk, bread, fruit...)
Tradutor: I usually buy eggs, meat, milk, fruit, and other things for the house.

🔸 Do you prefer going shopping alone or with someone?
👉 I prefer going alone / with my partner / with my family...
Tradutor: I prefer to go with my aunt to do the monthly shopping and alone to do the weekly shopping.
IPA: /'aɪ prɪ'fɜr tu ɡoʊ wɪð maɪ ænt tu du ðə 'mʌnθli 'ʃɑpɪŋ ənd ə'loʊn tu du ðə 'wiːkli 'ʃɑpɪŋ./
